# 🔪 A Shop for Killers (2024)

> A South Korean action-thriller series about survival, family, and secrets.

## 📌 About

*A Shop for Killers* is a 2024 Korean drama starring **Lee Dong-wook** and **Kim Hye-jun**. It follows Jeong Ji-an, who discovers the dangerous secrets behind her late uncle's mysterious shopping mall.
